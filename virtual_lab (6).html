Formulas & Origins
Thomas Young · 1801
Double-Slit Interference Intensity
I = I₁ + I₂ + 2√(I₁·I₂)·cos(δ)
δ = 2πx/w — phase difference from path length. Constructive where cos(δ)=1, destructive where cos(δ)=−1.
Albert Michelson · 1890
Fringe Visibility (Contrast)
V = (I_max − I_min) / (I_max + I_min)
Simplified to V = 2√(I₁·I₂)/(I₁+I₂). Equals 1.0 when both sources are equal. Noise degrades V toward 0.
Dennis Gabor · 1946
Coherence & Noise Degradation
V_eff = V · e^(−σ²/2)
σ = phase noise std. deviation. Exponential decay: small noise barely hurts, large noise obliterates fringes.
Wiener · 1930s
Signal-to-Noise Ratio
SNR = (I₁ + I₂) / σ_noise
When SNR < 1, noise dominates signal. Fringe pattern becomes indistinguishable from random static.
Fix: Filtering
Proposed Solution
I_clean = I * G(σ_filter)
Apply a Gaussian low-pass filter matched to fringe spatial frequency. Reduces σ, restores fringe visibility.
